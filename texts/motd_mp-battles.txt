1. Spawn is considered anywhere within a 5 second radius from where you spawn.
2. Spawnkilling is forbidden with LMGs, MGs, launchable grenades/explosives. (TNT and Geballte Ladung are allowed)
3. Spawnkilling is forbidden with officer call-ins.
4. Spamming with PIAT/explosive launchers using ammo crates is forbidden. Supply packs may be used for grenades, but not for spawnkilling.
5. Using exploits or ammo crates to leave the map is forbidden.
6. Defenders may destroy their own radio.
7. Attackers are allowed to destroy the enemy radio, but are encouraged to eliminate the remaining enemies.
8. Bayonetting in restricted areas is allowed.
9. Voice spamming with the intention to annoy other players is forbidden - it can reveal teammate positions.
10. Using the voice for gameplay purposes (officer/radio call, baiting, etc.) is allowed.
11. Racist or discriminatory language is forbidden and may result in a gag, or further punishment.
12. Apologise for teamkills - intentional teamkills are forbidden. (As the officer, let your teammates know when you're making call-ins to avoid teamkills.)
13. Abuse toward radio players is forbbiden, and using officer with fewer than 15 players is discouraged.
14. Intentional rule breaking will result in admin action, ranging from slays to bans depending on severity.

These rules are intended to keep the game enjoyable for both new and experienced players by preventing exploits and abusive behaviour. Players are also welcomed to join the DoI revival Revival Community for updates and events.
