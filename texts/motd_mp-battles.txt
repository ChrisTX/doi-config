1.  The spawn area is anywhere you can reach within 5 seconds of spawning.
2.  Spawnkilling with LMGs, MGs and launched grenades/explosives is forbidden. (TNT and Geballte Ladung are allowed.)
3.  Spawnkilling with officer call-ins is forbidden.
4.  Spamming PIATs or other explosive launchers by resupplying at ammo crates is forbidden. Supply packs may be used for grenades, but not for spawnkilling.
5.  Leaving the map through exploits or by using ammo crates is forbidden.
6.  Defenders may destroy their own radio.
7.  Attackers may destroy the enemy radio, but are encouraged to eliminate the remaining enemies instead.
8.  Bayonetting in restricted areas is allowed.
9.  Voice spamming to annoy other players is forbidden - it can also reveal your teammates' positions.
10. Using voice commands for gameplay (officer/radio calls, baiting, etc.) is allowed.
11. Racist or discriminatory language is forbidden and may result in a gag or further punishment.
12. Intentional teamkills are forbidden - apologise for accidental ones. (Officers: let your team know before a call-in to avoid teamkills.)
13. Abusing radiomen is forbidden, and playing officer with fewer than 15 players is discouraged.
14. Intentionally breaking the rules leads to admin action, from slays to bans depending on the severity.

These rules keep the game enjoyable for new and experienced players alike by preventing exploits and abusive behaviour. You are also welcome to join the DoI Revival Community for updates and events.
